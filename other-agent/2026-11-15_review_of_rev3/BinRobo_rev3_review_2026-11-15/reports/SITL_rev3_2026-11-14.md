# Verdict

**Revision 3 scores 48 / 60 on the frozen SITL criteria (ArduPilot Rover 4.6.2, frozen 50 Wh scorer).**
All 48 fault and hazard runs pass. All 12 full missions (s1a, s1b, s3a, s3b x seeds 7, 11, 23) complete, dock, place every
bin and are home before 21:00, and every one fails on **C6 only: 57.2 to 59.7 Wh per bin trip against the frozen 50 Wh limit.**
With only the C6 trip limit changed (every other criterion frozen): **60 / 60 at 65 Wh and 60 / 60 at 70 Wh.**
Raising the trip limit is a requirements change for Brian to accept or reject, not a design pass.

This confirms the other agent's 48/56 (their 8 fails are the same C6-only fails) and closes their gap: we ran all 60, including
the 4 missions they skipped (s3a s11/s23, s3b s7/s11) - all 4 complete, C6-only fail at 50 Wh, pass at 65/70 Wh.

Three things qualify it:

1. **No seating-check backstop in the SITL companion.** Their seating check after the lift (`X_POSTHOLD`, 30 mm) exists only in
   the MuJoCo mechanical model. In the SITL companion the lift is unconditional ("comb seated"). The only thing between a biased
   camera and a bad grab in SITL is the frozen pre-grab gate, which reads **ground truth** - a simulation oracle a real robot
   does not have. With a 60 mm camera bias the camera said "lined up" while the robot was 66 and 74 mm off; the oracle caught
   both. On hardware, as coded, both would have grabbed.
2. **Energy headroom at 65 Wh is thin.** Our worst trip is 59.7 Wh at 152.2 kg and 61.5 Wh at 162 kg; their 4.7.2 run had one
   64.3 Wh trip. A 65 Wh guard leaves 0.7 Wh over their worst. 70 Wh (worst x ~1.1) is the more defensible number if Brian
   accepts a change.
3. **New companion logic works but has two defects** (section 5): a refused trip is never closed (no trip energy, no "back on dock"
   time logged), and the camera loop applies each single noisy reading as a full correction.

Reading of the stress results:

- **Refusal + drive home works** in all 3 refusals (camA by the camera rule, camB and camC by the truth gate): the robot
  re-plans to lane -> dock and closes the dock contacts. No refusal left the robot in the lane.
- **Camera bias is invisible to the camera rule** (by construction: a biased camera reads "within 25 mm" when the robot is
  60-75 mm off). Only the truth gate caught it. See section 5.
- Night camera (camA): 1 of 2 refused; the refusal is driven as much by the 20 % no-reading rate as by noise. With noise sigma
  (25 mm) equal to the tolerance (25 mm), a perfectly aligned robot passes a single check only about half the time (about 52 %, including the 20 % no-reading rate); expect
  frequent refusals at night.
- All refused runs fail C3 in the frozen scorer because "last dock" is not recorded (defect D1), even though the robot docked.

# 5. Review of the new companion logic (diff vs rev 2)

The diff is limited to: `X_TRIP_WH`, `X_CAM_RNG`, `X_CAM_REFUSE` / `X_CAM_TRIES`, `X_FIX_RTH`, `X_CAM_BIAS` / `X_CAM_MISS`.
Runner unchanged (md5 identical to rev 2).

| # | item | finding | severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| OK | fresh camera error per check (`X_CAM_RNG`) | RNG seeded by (run seed, number of checks so far): independent per check and survives a checkpoint resume. Fixes the rev 2 re-seed defect. | fixed |
| OK | refusal after 3 re-alignments | loop runs up to 4 checks; refuses if the last check is off or has no reading. Behaves as described. | - |
| OK | drive home after refusal (`X_FIX_RTH`) | plan truncated to lane (if at pad) -> dock -> end; also applied to the truth-gate refusal. Verified in 3 runs. | - |
| D1 | refused trip never closed | the replacement plan omits `trip_end`, so the trip's Wh and the "back on dock" clock are never logged; the frozen scorer then fails C3 ("last dock None") although the robot docked. On hardware the app would show no return time and the energy log loses the trip. Add `('trip_end',)` before `('end', ...)`. | medium |
| D2 | single-reading full correction | every check moves the bin estimate by the full (noisy) camera reading; no averaging or gain. With 25 mm noise the estimate is pushed around (camA s1a: readings -53, +49, -68 mm; corrections +37 then -86 mm). Use a gain < 1 or average 2-3 frames. | medium |
| D3 | no-reading on the last try refuses | a single missing frame on the 4th check refuses even if the previous check was close. Acceptable as conservative; note it. | low |
| D4 | `refused` metric only on camera refusals | the truth-gate refusal is not added to `metrics['refused']`; reporting undercounts refusals. | low |
| D5 | acceptance band | camera tolerance 25 mm + 10 mm noise: a robot 38 mm off (truth) was accepted by the camera (m162, brown bin) - only 12 mm inside the 50 mm gate. | low |
| **B** | **seating-check backstop** | **not present in the SITL companion.** `X_POSTHOLD` is a MuJoCo-model switch only. After the final reverse the lift is unconditional and the bin is declared "seated". The pre-grab gate that stops bad grabs in SITL reads truth. | **high** |

Also noted: the final reverse onto the bin uses the **true** bin pose for its lateral correction (`lat_b` from world truth), so
final dock offsets (max 17 mm) are oracle-assisted, as in rev 2.

**Recommendation for the driving software:** (1) put a seating check in the companion (comb ToF / load-cell / lift-current
check after the lift, re-seat once, else set down and refuse) and re-run camB with the truth gate replaced by the camera
reading - that is the realistic test; (2) fix D1 and D2; (3) do not count C6 as passed - put the 65 vs 70 Wh question to Brian.
