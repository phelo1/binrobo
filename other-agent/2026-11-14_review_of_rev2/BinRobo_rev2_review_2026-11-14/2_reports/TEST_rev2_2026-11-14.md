# Verdict

**Revision 2 reproduces as claimed at the weight it was modelled at, but it does not pass at its real weight, and two lane conditions are harder than the report says.**

1. **At 119.39 kg (their table, 118.49 kg by the gate's count) the claims hold.** The gate passes 9 of 9, the results match their stored files digit for digit, and all 24 stress cases pass. Of 150 clip-lid pick-ups, 148 succeed and 2 are safe refusals. Every filled-pothole lane run passes, including P1 with the **sharp** 40 mm step, which they did not run.
2. **At the audited weight the gate fails G9.** The cold-eye audit gives about 125.6 kg by the gate's count. At that weight the robot still drives every test we ran (G1-G5, G8), but **G9 fails because 125.6 kg is over the 120 kg limit**, so the result is 8 of 9. The weight table, not the physics, is what fails.
3. **The pothole must be filled; that is a hard site rule, not an option.** With the 60 mm pothole open, P1 is scored PASS by the frozen lane rules, yet the robot is **stuck in the hole for 1,145 s** (19 minutes) and uses 50.6 Wh instead of 15.2 Wh. The frozen lane verdict has no time limit. With a 120 s stuck limit, P1, P5h and P7 all FAIL. No rocker or pin setting fixes this.
4. **Two of their statements are wrong in detail.**
    - "Never lifted a bin more than 31 mm off-centre" is false for the gate set. Trial s203 i=17 was lifted with the nib **50.6 mm** off-centre. That is 1 false hold under the ORIGINAL 50 mm band, and 0 under the 80 mm band they now use.
    - Their gate summary quotes G8 = 36.5 deg. That is B2-F's figure, read by mistake. Revision 2's own value is **34.7 deg** (still a pass).
5. **Simulator version matters.** On MuJoCo 3.17.0 a cylinder wheel meets a box edge as a vertical wall. G3 (the two-wheel step) then stalls at 1.34 m. Their numbers need MuJoCo 4.0.1, which reproduces them exactly. We treat 4.0.1 as the valid result and 3.17 as an artefact (section 5).

Nothing here is field data. Every number is from simulation (MuJoCo digital twin, frozen B2-F criteria).

# 2. Pick-ups (G1 clip-lid bin) at 119.39 kg

We re-ran their three seed sets, s303, s403 and s503, and added our own s603. Each set is 30 trials of the frozen G1 Monte Carlo. Every pick-up was logged with `simB2hx_r2m.py`, a logging-only wrapper with no change to the dynamics. It records, for each trial:

- the true sideways bin offset at every docking attempt;
- the nib's sideways position in the bin at the end of the grab;
- `seated()` re-evaluated with the ORIGINAL 640 mm comb band (50 mm on this bin) next to the 700 mm comb band (80 mm) the model now uses.

- **The results are identical to their stored files, trial for trial**, for s203, s303, s403 and s503.

# 3. The pothole time-limit loophole

The frozen lane verdict checks clearance, hedge, tip, seating and stall (12 s with no progress). It has **no limit on total time or energy**. A robot that keeps moving inside the pothole never trips the 12 s stall rule.

That is what happens in P1 with the pothole open:

- the robot reaches s 549 at t = 1,136 s;
- it slews sideways between lat 0 and 0.8 m in a limit cycle of about 450 s, with the hedge as close as 0.238 m;
- it escapes at t = 2,282 s, just before the 2,400 s drive timeout;
- the verdict is **PASS** after 50.6 Wh, against 15.2 Wh with the pothole filled.

**Tester re-score** (`work/rev2_vid/lane_timelimit.py` -> `/workspace/work/out/rev2/mech/lane_timelimit_r2b.json`). "Stuck" is the longest time the robot needs to gain 5 m of net chainage. This includes the grab, the set-down and planned stops, which are all under 60 s. Any run stuck for more than 120 s FAILS in practice. This rule is ours, not part of the frozen criteria.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Every filled-pothole run (P1, P2a, P2c, P3, P4a, P4b, P5a/c/d/f, P6a, P7, P1 sharp) | PASS | 14-58 | grab / set-down | PASS |
| P5g open trench | FAIL | 33 | s 455 | FAIL |
| P1, pothole open | **PASS** | **1,145** | s 550 | **FAIL** |
| P5h pothole stretch (snapshot of P1) | PASS | 1,145 | s 550 | FAIL |
| P7, pothole open | truncated | > 776, still stuck | s 550 | FAIL |
| P2a, pothole open (empty) | FAIL (stall s 550) | - | s 550 | FAIL |
| P2c, pothole open (13 kg bin) | truncated after crossing | 57 | grab | pass in practice |
| P5h, +3 kg | PASS | 162 | s 550 | FAIL |
| P5h on MuJoCo 3.17.0 | PASS | 15 | - | PASS (artefact, section 5) |

**Fix for the scoring:** add a per-run time limit, for example the filled-run time + 120 s, and an energy limit to the frozen lane criteria. Without them a trapped robot can score PASS.

# 9. Caveats and what was not run

- **Not run:**
    - the B2-F S12 comparison (only G3 one side was run, as instructed when time was short);
    - the +3 kg proxy full gate;
    - the full 3.17 gate (A/B only);
    - G6/G7 and lane runs at the audited mass;
    - pothole-open P2c to the end (truncated after a clean crossing).
- **The pothole sensitivity runs were truncated** while still stuck. They are lower bounds, already over 120 s.
- **Chaos.** Docking and pothole escape change outcome with small changes: s203 i=14 refuses at 119.4 kg but succeeds at 126.5 kg, and some pothole runs trap while others escape. Single runs are not statistics. Only the pick-up counts (150 trials) are.
- **The time-limit rule** (stuck over 120 s = FAIL) is our addition, not a frozen criterion.
- **The audited mass is the cold-eye's central estimate.** The range is 122.8-127.7 kg, and every value in the range fails G9.
- **No structural check was made here.** Strength (B-2 in the cold-eye) is not covered by these simulations.
- **The 12 % pitch and the feature chainages are assumed overlays** on the lane profile, as in B2-F.
