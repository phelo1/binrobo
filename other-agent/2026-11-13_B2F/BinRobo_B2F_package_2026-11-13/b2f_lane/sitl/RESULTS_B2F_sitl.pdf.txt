Plain reading: on this simulated lane the B2-F stack does the one-bin drop reliably and every safety behaviour works,
but it is not yet good enough for unattended pickups or three-bin evenings: pickups fail about 1 time in 2
at the line-up step, and the three-bin drop fails every time because of one path-planning bug near the house.

5. Failures and root causes
RC-A, s3a on all seeds (deterministic). The 140 L brown bin’s home spot is at lateral -4.6 m in the yard.
lane() holds the start lateral until s 12, then moves the lateral target at no more than 0.12 m/s. The loaded robot
is therefore still at lateral -3.3 m at s 23-25.7, where the lane hedge starts. The footprint came within 0.012 / 0.071
/ 0.033 m of the truth hedge (C1 < 0.124 m), and the firmware fence (polygon 1.6 m inside the hedge line) switched
to HOLD (C7). C3 and C6 fail as a consequence because the mission stopped after 3 minutes. The 240 L black bin
(lateral -1.4 m) never shows this, which is why s1a passes.
RC-B, pickups (s1b seeds 11 and 23, s3b seeds 7 and 23). The truth pre-grab gate (lateral <= 0.05 m)
missed twice, so the robot correctly refused to grab. The Pi refines the bin pose once, from the staging pose 4.5-8 m
away and off the bin axis, in the EKF frame. An EKF yaw error of 0.4-0.9 deg (= 0.03-0.12 m at that range) plus
the median of an obliquely seen front face gave refine errors of 0.03-0.12 m. Examples: s1b s11 refine +0.085 m vs
truth +0.009 m, s3b s7 +0.098 vs -0.020 m. The retry re-uses the same estimate. s3b seed 11 passed only after one
miss (-0.060 m, retry -0.031 m). C3, C6 and C8 failures in these runs are consequences.
RC-C, s4b seed 23. Sign error in lane(): the branch for “only the right hedge face visible, too close to it”
computes the wanted left move as -(KL_GAP+0.6-(dR-hw)), which steers toward the right hedge. With RTK lost
at s 60 and the left-hedge field-gate gap at s 60-64, the robot was pushed onto the right hedge (0.000 m). The Pi
dead-reckoning limit then held it. The EKF without GPS drifted 2-3.5 m and the firmware fence tripped on that
estimate (the true boundary margin was fine). It shows only when the robot runs in a left-side gap without RTK.
Scorer note. C8 is computed inconsistently for runs whose gate misses did not lead to a grab: s3b s23 shows C8
PASS, while s1b s11 / s23 and s3b s7 show C8 FAIL. No verdict changes, because C7 fails all of these runs. The
scorer is reported as frozen.


6. Key measurements (truth)
   • Hedge clearance (C1 >= 0.124 m): all moving runs except RC-A / RC-C at 0.196-0.413 m. Full-lane
     missions 0.196-0.256 m. The tightest points are s ~417 (the assumed overgrown right-hedge bulge, loaded
     up-lane) and s ~502 (assumed narrowing with 2.9 m between hedges). Pad A: 0.38-0.41 m.
   • Set-down (C2 <= 0.15 m, <= 10 deg): 15 of 15 set-downs passed, 0.006-0.054 m and 0.1-6.6 deg.
   • Bye-law clock (C3): one-bin trips 18:00 -> 18:56-18:57. Three-bin pickup (s3b s11) 18:00 -> 20:47, leaving
     13 min of the 21:00 limit. The three-bin evening has very little time to spare.
   • Energy (C6 <= 50 Wh per trip): 44.7-46.7 Wh per trip, about 7-10 % under the limit. The three-bin
     end SOC is above 20 %. The low-battery guard refused the trip on all seeds.
   • Failsafes: e-stop 0.038-0.048 m (<= 0.30). Companion freeze: HOLD after 1.56-1.80 s (<= 2.5), rolled
     0.58-0.88 m loaded (<= 1.0, smallest margin 0.12 m) and 0.26-0.27 m while reversing. Firmware fence HOLD
     with 0.62-0.65 m to the boundary. Car at rest 2.33-2.36 m (>= 0.50). No contact with any obstacle or bin in
     any run.
   • Pre-grab gate (C8): 14 OK at the first attempt, 1 OK on the retry, 9 missed attempts; 4 bins were never
     grabbed (RC-B).

9. Limits of this evidence
   • It is a simulation of an assumed lane. Every number above depends on the assumed hedges, canopy, boundary
     and pads in section 7.
   • SITL is not fully deterministic between runs (see the voided s3b s7 attempt). Three seeds give an indication,
     not a failure rate.
   • s5a never needed a full stop: the person and dog crossed clear while the robot slowed to 0.25 m/s, so the
     at-rest person gap (C5b) was not exercised. s5b exercised it with the car.
   • Grab and lift come from the MuJoCo gate. The lidar, RTK, odometry and energy models are stand-ins.


10. Fixes suggested by the failures (not applied; a new frozen run would
be needed)
   1. RC-A: plan the yard exit as a path to the lane entry pose (s 20, lat 0) instead of a lateral ramp, or remove the
      0.12 m/s limit inside the yard.
   2. RC-B: re-measure the bin face from the pre-grab pose (1.2 m, on axis) before the gate, and re-measure again
      on the retry. Optionally take the lateral from the rear camera.
   3. RC-C: flip the sign of the right-face keep-off branch in lane(), and add a unit test for it.
   4. Timing: the three-bin evening uses about 165 of 180 minutes. Consider 0.5 m/s on straights or a shorter
      staging sequence.
