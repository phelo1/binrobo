1. Summary
Independent cold-eye rebuild and test of the “improved version”: B2-F plus (a) a fixed pad, (b) 350 mm lift travel on a
mast 60 mm taller, (c) nose castors on a centre-pivot rocker beam, pin at 200 mm. The other agent’s simulator was
not available, so the version was rebuilt from their stated changes (h2/make_simB2hx.py -> h2/simB2hx.py, every
edit an auditable string replacement on the unchanged B2-F sim). Same frozen criteria, seeds and scorer as B2-F
(h2/summarize_h2.py). No failure is hidden.
Headline results (pin 200 mm, sim mass 96.94 kg unless stated):
   • Frozen gate: 7 of 9 (B2-F re-scored from its own files: 8 of 9). FAIL G1 (88/90, 2 false holds) and FAIL G3
     (step “both” stalls, as in B2-F). The other agent claims 9 of 9 at pin 200 mm.
   • G1 at pin 287 mm: 87/90, 2 false holds -> FAIL (their claim 86/90, 2 false holds -> FAIL: same verdict).
   • G1 is mass-sensitive: ESE240CL 30/30 at 92.32 kg (their mass), 29/30 (1 false hold) at 95.74 kg, 28/30 (2
     false holds) at 96.94 kg. The drawn version weighs about 94-95 kg (cold-eye review), so the 90/90 claim only
     holds at the outdated 92.32 kg.
   • Lane: P1 FAILS with the step ramped and with the step sharp - stall in the 60 mm pothole at s 550.6
     m. B2-F P1 passes. Cause isolated: the free rocker. Same start, rocker locked -> PASS; rocker free at 92.32 kg
     or at +/-3 deg stops -> FAIL. The other agent claims P1, P2a, P2c and P7 pass.
   • Sharp 40 mm step: passed going DOWN loaded (lift at the 3 kN cap); going UP (P5d, 3 kN) stalls at s 290.1,
     as in B2-F.
   • S12 (load envelope incl. B2-F pothole/kerb runs): mast foot SF 0.68; side rail edge 0.39; rail block SF
     2.29 at 200 mm pitch and 1.37 at the drawn 120 mm; rail screws 1.10 / 0.66. Rocker pin bush SF 1.88 (pin
     200 mm) / 1.31 (287 mm); hanger plate 1.63.
   • Castor vs bumper (logged in the sim, 141 runs): wheel overlaps the bumper by 41.6 mm in every docking
     run, and the 45 deg bumper corners by 19.3 mm. This confirms review finding B-1 (45.4 mm by CAD).
   • Perturbations: 23 of 24 pass; mass x1.1 grab ESE240SL 4/5 with 1 false hold (B2-F 5/5).
   • Lane fit (plan geometry): PASS, margins as B2-F (pivot loaded 2.41 m vs 2.39 m; narrowing pivot margin 0.171
     m vs 0.181 m).

Pothole diagnosis (P5h, same start state):

                                      variant                                      result
                                      96.94 kg, rocker free +/-6 deg               FAIL
                                      92.32 kg, rocker free +/-6 deg               FAIL
                                      96.94 kg, roll stops +/-3 deg                FAIL
                                      96.94 kg, rocker locked (+/-0.05 deg)        PASS


In this model the free rocker, not the mass, is what loses the pothole. With the rocker free, the chassis rolls onto the
castor beam when the left drive wheel drops 60 mm, and the drive wheels lose the load they need for traction. Possible
fixes are a stiff roll spring or a much tighter stop (well under +/-3 deg), which needs a check against the kerb and twist
cases the rocker was added for, or filling the pothole on site (the lane runs already carry the filled variant).
