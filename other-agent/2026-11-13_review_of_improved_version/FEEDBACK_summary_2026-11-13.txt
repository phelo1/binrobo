Feedback on "BinRobo improved version" (B2-F + fixed pad, 350 mm lift, castor rocker at 200 mm), 13 Nov 2026

From: independent reviewer (cold-eye drawing review plus an independent re-test).

Scope and basis
• Your simB2hx.py was not supplied. We rebuilt the three changes as auditable string edits on the unchanged B2-F sim:
  - lift_range 0.35, mast and rails +60 mm;
  - pad_ret 0, lift_extra1 0.15, B2F_PAD_IN -0.005 (same as your commands);
  - rocker pin X_ROCK_Z 0.200 / 0.287;
  - soft roll stops at +/-6 deg, damping 2 N m s/rad, friction 0.5 N m, beam 4.83 kg.
• We used the same frozen criteria, seeds and scorer as B2-F.
• Clash numbers are OpenCascade booleans on your unchanged cad_B2F_src.py plus your build_model.py edits, posed from rec_B2F.npz.
• Please send your simB2hx.py, or its diff against simB2h.py, so the discrepancies below can be traced to the model rather than argued.

PART A. Your claims we could NOT reproduce

A1. Gate at pin 200 mm: you report 9/9; we get 7/9.
• G1 pick-ups: 88/90, with 2 false holds on ESE240CL seeds 14 and 17, so FAIL.
• G1 depends on mass. ESE240CL gives:
  - 30/30 at 92.32 kg (your mass);
  - 29/30 with 1 false hold at 95.74 kg;
  - 28/30 with 2 false holds at 96.94 kg.
  Your 90/90 only holds at the old B2-F mass.
• G3 40 mm full-width step "both": we stall at 1.34 m at all three masses (92.32, 95.74, 96.94 kg), exactly as B2-F. You reach 4.71 m with the lift at 3,000 N. Your "one" run also shows 2.9 deg roll against our 0.26 deg.
• Please check:
  (a) how the rocker joint, stops and damping are modelled;
  (b) whether the step geometry or the wheel torque cap (60 N m) differ from the B2-F frozen setup;
  (c) whether G9 and the mass tables were updated. Both your gate files still report 92.32 kg.

A2. Lane runs: you report P1, P2a, P2c and P7 passing with the step ramped. Our P1 fails in the 60 mm pothole at s 550.6 m, both with the step ramped and with it sharp.
• We ran the pothole stretch from the same start state (P5h) four ways:
  - 96.94 kg, rocker free (+/-6 deg): FAIL
  - 92.32 kg, rocker free: FAIL
  - 96.94 kg, stops at +/-3 deg: FAIL
  - 96.94 kg, rocker locked: PASS
• Our reading: when one drive wheel drops 60 mm, the chassis rolls onto the free rocker and the drive wheels lose the normal load they need for traction.
• Please check:
  (a) your rocker roll stiffness and stops in the lane world;
  (b) whether your lane world has the 60 mm pothole at s about 550;
  (c) the drive-wheel normal loads through the pothole, rocker free vs locked.
• P2a, P2c and P7 could not be run here because P1 never reaches pad A. Note that B2-F's own P2a, P2c and P7 also stall at that pothole.

A3. Sharp 40 mm step up (P5d, 3 kN): stalls at s 290.1, the same as B2-F. Going down loaded (P5a) passes, but the lift hits the 3 kN cap (2,925 N).

PART B. Where we agree
• Pin at 287 mm is worse: our G1 is 87/90 with 2 false holds (FAIL); yours is 86/90 with 2 false holds (FAIL). Keeping 200 mm is right.
• Caveat on that comparison: 26/30 vs 30/30 gives a Fisher two-sided p of about 0.11, so the pass-rate difference alone is weak. The rise in lateral arrival error (15.8 to 24.6 mm mean on ESE240CL) is the stronger evidence.
• Our hypothesis for the cause:
  - A lateral castor force on a 43% longer lever moves more vertical load between the castors.
  - Contact-patch side play at the stop is h x theta: 15.8 mm at 200 mm vs 22.8 mm at 287 mm.
• Your bay floor, wall and ballast vs castor-swivel finding is confirmed:
  - Overlap per side: floor 385 cm3, walls 19.9 cm3, ballast 38 mm.
  - Your trims clear it. In the sim the closest approaches are floor 7.9 mm, ballast 16.7 mm and walls 32.7 mm.
  - The mass saving checks out at 1.21 kg. The shortened front cover saves another 0.29 kg you did not count.
• Your bumper overlap finding is confirmed: 45.4 mm by CAD, and 41.6 mm logged in the sim in every reversing dock (141 runs). See B-1 below for why it is a blocker, not an open issue.
• Lane fit (plan geometry) passes, with margins as B2-F: loaded pivot 2.41 m vs 2.39 m. This uses the drawn nose; a 60-65 mm bumper move would reduce the margins.
• SITL (kinematic): 53/60 vs B2-F 52/60, which is within seed noise. Two margins get thinner:
  - energy per trip: 48.1 of 50 Wh, against 46.7 for B2-F;
  - latest dock on a three-bin evening: 20:50, against 20:47.

PART C. Blockers in the drawn version

B-1. The castors hit the front bumper on every dock.
• B2-F docks by reversing, which leaves the castors swivelled forward on every approach. Your get_poses.py says the same.
• With the castor forward, the wheel front reaches x 1095.4 at the bumper's lower edge (z 130). The bumper back face is at x 1050.
• With the rocker at its stop (4.8 deg) the rising castor fills the whole 40 mm bumper depth (296 cm3). The 45 deg corners overlap by a further 19.3 mm.
• The 260 mm castor option is worse:
  - forward overlap of 95 mm, not your stated +45;
  - its swivel radius of 197 mm reaches y 477, beyond the 465 mm half-width;
  - inward, it reaches the ballast, floor tongue and battery;
  - it needs about 300-310 mm of build height under a 260 mm plate.
• Fix, choose one together with the castor size:
  - Bumper back face at x >= 1110: the robot becomes about 1,566-1,572 mm long, and the nose is already 16 mm over the 1.09 m spec. Re-run the SITL hedge, yard-exit and docking checks.
  - Castors about 60 mm back, wheelbase 885-890 mm: the bay side walls must end at x <= 725, since the envelope reaches x 733. Re-run all mechanical tests.

B-2. No FDI 4 kN unit fits 350 mm of travel.
• From the base pin (z about 410) to the rod end at q = 0 there is about 560 mm.
• FDI 4 kN, 6 mm/s, from anodas.lt on 13 Nov 2026:
  - 300 mm stroke: L = 500 mm. It fits, but gives only 300 mm of travel.
  - 400 mm stroke: L = 620 mm, 60 mm too long. Its internal limits cannot be adjusted and it has no feedback.
• Run to its own end stop, the 400 mm unit drives the carriage to z 1400, 30 mm into the top tie (underside 1370). The rail blocks end at 1390, 30 mm past the rail ends (1360).
• The drawn actuator body already sits 45 mm inside the carriage bracket at q = 0 (84 cm3).
• Loom L5 lists a "position signal" that this unit does not have.
• Fix:
  - Lower the base pin by at least 60 mm (about z 350); the rear cover needs a cut-out.
  - Fit a hard stop at 350 mm plus an external top limit switch, or use a feedback actuator.
  - Change BOM line B-0 to the 400 mm unit. It currently reads "300 mm 4 kN, priced as the 400 mm unit".
• Cycle time: 350 mm at 6 mm/s takes 58 s per lift, against 42 s.
• Note for our own rebuild: the sim assumed the actuator covers 350 mm at the same 3 kN cap, so the sim is optimistic here.

B-3. The S12 structural fixes are not drawn (carried over from B2-F), and the drawn rail-block pitch makes it worse.
• Mast upright foot, 50x50x2: SF 0.68 at comb_all (9,634 N). The 350 mm travel does not change it, since M = F_comb x 0.425 m is independent of carriage height.
• Side rail 50x50x2: SF 1.10 carry, 0.39 edge.
• Drawn block pitch is 120 mm, not the 200 mm S12 assumed:
  - HGH15 block load 17,075 N instead of 10,245 N, so SF on C0 falls from 2.29 to 1.37;
  - M4 rail screws: SF falls from 1.10 to 0.66.
• Fix:
  - draw 80x40x4 uprights and 50x50x4 rails (the edge case needs 80x80x5, which is still open);
  - block pitch >= 200 mm, rail screws M4 12.9 into a 10 mm backing flat;
  - re-run S12.

PART D. Major findings

M-1. Looms through moving parts (boolean overlaps):
• L2/L3 encoder branch through the rotating drive tyre and hub: 9.4 + 21.9 cm3 per side.
• L7 carriage branches across both HGR15 rails, and its fixed run through the moving rail block and the keeper motor.
• L4 through the gearmotor bodies: 12.3 cm3 per side.
• Fix: re-route. L7 needs a mast-anchored drag chain with a service loop sized for 350 mm.

M-2. The e-stop and fusing chain is incomplete.
• No loom from the pod to the power box for:
  - the KM1/KM2 contactor coils from the PNOZ outputs;
  - the contactor EDM feedback;
  - the SLS voltage sense (BOM PC-G7 is not drawn).
• The tilt switch (S13) and the SLS-L8 box are not placed.
• The lift actuator has no driver. S6 put it on Roboteq ch3, but CTL-L8 is a 2-channel RoboClaw used by the drive. Nothing shows that the e-stop removes lift power, and the lift carries a bin overhead with a 20 mm top gap.
• The main fuse is drawn in the power box, but BOM S1 says at battery +, which leaves about 0.22 m of unfused 10 mm2 trunk.
• The charge pair from the exposed bumper contacts is unfused; the G16 10 A fuse is not placed.
• The S2 fuse box is not placed.
• Please supply a B2-F wiring table covering protection devices, segregation, the actuator driver and its e-stop interlock.

M-3. The crush gap from carriage top (z 1350 at q = 350) to the top-tie underside (1370) is 20 mm, under the 25 mm finger minimum (ISO 13854). Stop at <= 340 mm or guard the gap.

M-4. Fixed pad.
• At q = 0 the pad face (x 284.8) stands 0.2 mm proud of the nib tip (285.0), with 300 N preload at 30 kN/m. The pad touches first and pushes the bin before the nib seats. The bin rolls away at about 30-55 N.
• On release it scrubs about 100 mm down the front wall at about 110 N (540 N x mu 0.2).
• The CAD gives 1.2 mm free gap face to cup; at carry the face is drawn 6.7 mm through the cup plate. The sim models a 40 mm stroke.
• Your note says "pad 5 mm further back", but pad_in changed from 0.015 to -0.005, which is 20 mm. Please confirm the sign convention.
• Fix:
  - a real >= 45 mm sprung stroke;
  - pad >= 10 mm behind the nib, or a passive cam retract;
  - test with the bin brake on and off, and on Brian's real bins.

M-5. False holds.
• Your two 287 mm false holds lifted the bin 34 and 72 mm off-centre while the switches reported a good hold. We also see false holds at 200 mm when the mass is realistic.
• Hold detection cannot see lateral seating.
• Fix:
  - add a lateral-seating check, e.g. both comb ToFs within a band;
  - run >= 100 seeds on ESE240CL at 200 mm.

M-6. Mass.
• "About 92 kg" is the B2-F figure, and both of your pivot-height gate files report G9 = 92.32 kg.
• Recomputed from the CAD solids it is about 94-95 kg (net +1.7 to +2.7 kg). With the S12 sections it is about 113-114 kg.
• Please update the mass model and re-run the force-limited tests (3 kN lift cap, 60 N m wheel cap).

M-7. Parts list (sheet 13) vs BOM:
• "RK-1" does not exist, so the castor beam, pin, hangers, bushes and stops are uncosted.
• Item 27 says 400 mm, but BOM B-0 says 300 mm.
• The fixed pad should delete B2-PADACT (EUR 59.99), B2-TOGGLE (EUR 12) and the B1-PADLS lock use, about EUR 80 in all.
• The ballast (item 46) has no reference. The BOM has BT-01 (jerricans + sand) and B-6, and neither matches the 5 kg plate.
• Wrong references: item 17 should be G12, not FAB-B2; items 38-39 should be B-3.
• Missing: G16, HTR-L8 and the front camera line.

PART E. Minor findings
• Legacy CAD clashes:
  - UHMW guard 3 mm into the drive tyres (92.5 cm3 per side);
  - encoder box into the guard and hub;
  - keeper motor (item 36) through the left rail and upright;
  - sensor post D40 through the lidar (86 cm3);
  - pad arm inside the comb pivot block, so the "self-aligning" comb cannot yaw or roll as drawn.

  - Hanger plate SF is 1.63 (our S12 lever) or 2.9 (review lever). Please state your lever.
  - Until a stop touches, the nose sits on one point. Static lateral margin when empty drops from 338 to 186 mm.
• Wording:
  - the "304 mm across" swivel room is 295 mm at the bumper's lower edge;
  - the sheet 14 loom table cuts off the L9 route text.
