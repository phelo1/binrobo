BinRobo revision 4 - independent re-test (mechanical gate, person stop,
                 lowering, steps, lane), MuJoCo 4.0.1
                                      Tester report, 21 November 2026




1. Verdict
Everything revision 4 reports reproduces. Every point of their person-stop table that I re-ran (16 cells plus
their side cases) matches to the millimetre; one wet-leaf side case differs by 2 mm. Their lowering, step and lane
verdicts match too, and so do the gate pick-up files (179 of 180 captured, 1 safe refusal). Sim mass is 177.60 kg, CG
x 0.497 m / z 0.475 m from the axle, as they state. The lane run P1 takes 1834 s and 16.7 Wh, against their 1833.6
s and 16.67 Wh.
The headline “longest simulated stop 122 mm against 150 mm” does not hold for the robot as it
would really drive. It holds only inside their scoring assumptions:
  • The empty robot on the 12 % pitch is scored tail first (71 mm), but tail-first driving does not
    exist. It is not in the driving software and not in the lane runs. Nose first, their own table gives 389 mm at
    0.2 m/s. I get 389 mm too.
  • Slope transitions are not in the map. The speed rule looks at the slope the robot is on, not the slope
    ahead. Suppose the empty robot is doing 0.4 m/s on the level (allowed) when the ground tips into the 12 %
    pitch and the bumper is touched. It needs 938 mm and 6.7 s to stop. At +10 % mass (187 kg pstop mass) it
    needs 1098 mm and has still not stopped after 12 s. Full bin, same case: 191 mm. Other transitions,
    empty: 9 -> 12 % at 0.2 m/s: 329 mm; 3 -> 9 % at 0.3 m/s: 156 mm.
  • Castor brakes do NOT fix wet leaves. Revision 4 recommends castor brakes as the fix for empty-robot
    braking. On mu 0.30 they work: 46 mm at 0.2 m/s, and 167 mm at 0.5 m/s (= theirs). On wet leaves (mu
    0.22, 12 %, empty) they give 644 mm from 0.2 m/s and 753 mm from 0.5 m/s, 4-5 times the 150 mm.
    Their own section 7 mentions an unexplained 0.72 m run. It was not a fluke; it is the result. Without castor
    brakes, the empty robot on wet leaves does not stop at all (13.2 m in 8 s). It is already sliding at 0.87 m/s
    when the bumper is touched, because the drive wheels cannot hold it at 0.2 m/s.
  • The lowering guard threshold is about 22 mm, not “about 20 mm”. They ran 15 mm (not seen) and
    25 mm (seen) only. I ran 20 mm: not seen at 6 and at 13 mm/s, for 110 kg and 25 kg bins and under both
    wheels. The bin was set down on the block (837-1180 N on it). Still not seen at +10 % mass (815 N). 30 mm
    trips at 107 N.
  • Open pothole: the hedge clearance falls to 5.6 mm. P5h stalls at s 550.5, as in their file. While working
    at the hole the body passes 5.6 mm from the hedge line (s 550.0). Their reply does not mention this.
  • The empty 40 mm step fails on B2-F as well. Empty at 3.0 m run-up, 0.3 and 0.5 m/s: stall. The boost
    never fires (n = 0, the wheels spin). This answers their question 1 to the B2-F author: the empty-step problem
    belongs to the whole layout (two driven rear wheels, castors in front), not to revision 4.
Overall: as a simulation, revision 4 is reproducible and honest about most of its limits. Its own text says the empty
robot brakes badly and that tail first is not implemented. The summary and the 122 mm headline are still more
favourable than the results. As a design for the assumed 12 % wet lane, the empty robot still cannot be stopped
within the bumper travel, with or without castor brakes. The 150 mm bumper travel itself is an assumption (half its
depth; the maker has not confirmed it). The real lane slope is unmeasured (the map data says at most 7.9 %), and
it decides most of this.

4. What their scoring hides
  1. Tail first is assumed, not built. The “122 mm longest stop” uses the tail-first value (71 mm) for the empty
     robot on > 9 %. Their mission runs drive it nose first at 0.15 m/s, which gives 289 mm.
  2. The map has only steady slopes. Every map cell starts on a constant grade. The worst real moment is
     the brow of the pitch: the robot is on the level at the level speed and the ground tips under it. Empty, 938
     mm; at +10 % mass, 1098 mm and still not stopped. A speed rule driven by a look-ahead (map position or
     IMU pitch rate) would be needed.
  3. Wet leaves are an aside. In their text: “Empty on 12 %: it does not stop at all.” It is not in the headline,
     and the castor-brake recommendation is presented as fixing empty braking. On mu 0.22 it does not (644-753
     mm).
  4. “About 20 mm” for the lowering guard is an interpolation between 15 mm (not seen) and 25 mm
     (seen). At 20 mm it is not seen. The bin is put down on the object with up to 1180 N on it.
  5. The lane pass is on a lane with its three rough spots made good. On the lane as modelled: stall at
     the step up, in the trench and in the pothole. The pothole stall also brings the robot within 5.6 mm of the
     hedge. The 12 s stuck rule ends those runs early. With a 120 s limit the made-good runs still pass (P2a, P2c).
  6. The boost does not help the sharp step in the lane. P5d with X_BOOST=100 still stalls at s 290.1 (lift
     2169 N). It helps only in the gate test with 3.0 m of run-up at 0.5 m/s.

5. New findings (not in their reply)
  • Wet leaves, empty, 12 %, castor brakes: 644 mm at 0.2 m/s, 753 mm at 0.5 m/s. Rev 4 without them:
    no stop (13.2 m in 8 s). B2-F empty on mu 0.22: 1145 / 1164 mm, but it stops and holds 0.2 m/s before the
    touch. B2-F on mu 0.30: 108 mm (rev 4: 289 / 389).
  • Slope transitions: empty, level -> 12 % at 0.4 m/s: 938 mm (6.7 s); +10 % mass: 1098 mm, not
    stopped at 12 s. Full bin, same case: 191 mm. Empty 9 -> 12 % at 0.2: 329 mm. Empty 3 -> 9 % at 0.3:
    156 mm.
  • Reaction 0.20 s: full 9 % at 0.3 m/s: 136 mm (rule speed; their 0.20 s probe was level only). Grip 0.22 at
    the empty 9 % rule speed (0.2 m/s): 334 mm.
  • Lowering: 20 mm not seen at 6 and 13 mm/s, 110 / 25 kg bins, both wheels, +10 % mass. Estimated
    threshold about 22 mm (15 mm margin + about 7 mm offset). 30 mm trips at 107 N (25 kg bin: 83 N).
  • Empty 40 mm step: stalls on B2-F too (0.3 and 0.5 m/s, 3.0 m run-up). It is a layout problem.
  • Sharp drop P5a: passes, but the robot reaches 0.703 m/s over the edge. That is 1.76 times the 0.4 m/s
    rule cap, so the speed rule does not hold on a drop. Wheel clearance min 47.4 mm, lift peak 2874 N.
  • Open pothole P5h: hedge clearance 5.6 mm, lift 2348 N, roll 3.8 deg.
