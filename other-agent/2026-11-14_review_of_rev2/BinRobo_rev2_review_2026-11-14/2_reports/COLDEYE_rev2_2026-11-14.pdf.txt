Cold-eye review: BinRobo revision 2 drawing set (15 A3 sheets, 14 Nov 2026)


                                                14 November 2026


Verdict
Better than revision 1, still not a build basis. Revision 2 really does close the castor-bumper clash (B-1), the loom
routing (M-1) and most of the actuator fit (B-2). Every figure below is from OpenCascade booleans or a hand calculation,
not from the other agent’s wording. Two Blockers remain, one carried over and one new:
  1. The lift top end does not work as drawn (new). The carriage plate runs into the body of the top limit switch
     at q = 300 mm, only 10.6 mm above the carry height. It then hits the “345 mm” hard stops at q = 336 mm, before
     the 340 mm switch point. The hard stops are rail clamps. A size-15 rail clamp holds 1,200 N, so two hold 2,400 N
     against a 4,000 N actuator. If the switch fails, the actuator’s internal end stop is at q = 388 mm, which drives the
     carriage plate 18 mm into the top tie.
  2. Structure (B-3, partly closed). The drawn sections fix the upright and the block pitch. Two load cases still fail:
       • The side rail in the edge case: SF 0.69 with 50x50x4. The CAD still draws a 2 mm wall.
       • A load path the S12 calculation never checked: each upright’s foot moment goes into the 50x50x2 mast-base
          cross-member as torsion, SF 0.58 at comb_all.
     Several parts are drawn with no joint to the frame: the rocker hanger plates (6 mm clear), the bumper brackets
     (they touch on one edge only) and the mast gussets (nothing underneath).
There are 6 Major findings:
  • The weight is about 123-128 kg by the gate’s count, not 118.5 kg. That is over the 120 kg limit.
  • The stop chain is a block diagram, not a PL d design.
  • The pad springs as drawn cannot give 45 mm of stroke.
  • The bumper brackets are a concept only, weak sideways, and give no travel for the bumper switches.
  • Parts list vs BOM: 14 open mismatches, about 9 unpriced items.
  • The actuator: whether it self-locks is unconfirmed, and it has no position signal.
Revision 2 costs about EUR 0 to +200 more than B2-F including S12. That is about EUR 4,250-4,450 all-in with
the new drive.
Scope and method.
  • Model. I rebuilt the revision 2 model from the other agent’s build_model.py (unchanged copy) on the unchanged
    B2-F cad_B2F_src.py.
  • Poses. These came from a fresh grab run with their own revision 2 switches, using get_poses.py with the paths
    changed. The run used h2/simB2hx_r2.py, which is generated by their make_simB2hx.py, and their r2 mass table.
    The grab succeeded: lift at carry q = 289.4 mm, bin wheels 95.9 mm clear.
  • Checks. Booleans and minimum distances were done with OCP BRepAlgoAPI_Common and BRepEx-
    trema_DistShapeShape.
  • Not run. No MuJoCo or SITL test was re-run here. Those claims belong to the separate test stream.
  • Files. Scripts and raw results are in /workspace/work/coldeye_rev2/:
       – get_poses_r2.py, model_parts_r2.py, clash_r2.py, env_r2.py, lift_r2.py, loom_r2.py, attach.py,
         s12_rev2.py, mass_r2.py, figs.py;
       – the JSON and MD outputs clash_imp_r2.json, env_r2.json, lift_r2.json, loom_r2_*.json,
         s12_rev2.json and s12_rev2.md.

  • The actuator geometry is fine as drawn:
       – The base pin is at about z 338 and the rod eye at z 970 at q = 0. That is 632 mm pin to pin against the
          supplier’s L = 620 mm, so 12 mm extended at q = 0.
       – The actuator’s own end stop is therefore at q of about 388 mm. There the plate top is at 1388 mm, against
          the tie underside at 1370 mm.
       – The base sits above the rear cover (z 318 or more against 306), so no cover cut-out is needed.
  • The hard stops do not hold. Item 67 is “clamped on the rail”. A manual clamp for a size-15 profile rail is rated
    1,200 N holding force, per the Zimmer HK1501A datasheet: https://www.zimmer-group.com/swk-datasheet/NSK-
    SHL1H-15-SAHANZ-HK1501A-EN.pdf. Two clamps give 2,400 N against the 4,000 N actuator (SF 0.60), so the
    stop slips.
  • The switch is the only working overrun protection. It is single-channel in the up feed, and it is drawn
    floating 4.5 mm off the upright with no bracket.
Fix:
  • Move the switch and the stops outboard of the plate (y > 232), or notch the plate.
  • Make the hard stop a bolted block through the rail-backing flat into the upright, rated at least 2 x the actuator
    stall force. The 4 kN rating is not the stall force; ask the supplier.
  • Set the stops so the blocks land at 340 mm at most. That keeps 30 mm under the tie and the 25 mm finger
    minimum.
  • Better: fit a 350 mm-stroke actuator so its own internal limit is the hard limit.
  • Re-check that the 400 mm unit’s internal top limit cannot reach the tie.

M-2. Stop chain (sheet 15): a sound block diagram, not yet a PL d design
What is right:
  • The E-stop goes to a two-channel relay (BOM SR-L8, a Pilz PNOZ X3, used).
  • KM1 and KM2 are in series, each coil fed by its own relay channel.


                                                           4
  • There is a feedback loop to the monitored reset.
  • The lift driver is fed from the contactor-switched bus, so any stop removes lift power. That is the interlock asked for.
  • The main fuse is now drawn on the battery positive (item 65, x 734-784, on the pack).
  • The 10 A charge fuse and ideal diode are in the power box, with the charge pair in L9.
  • L11 now carries coils, feedback and voltage sense from the pod to the power box.
Missing or wrong:
  1. Not two-channel throughout, despite the note:
       • The tilt switch S13 is a single ball switch.
       • The BMP-L8 bumpers use Omron D4N-4120 switches, which are 1NC/1NO. The front bar can be two-channel,
          but each rear corner bumper has one NC contact.
       • The two watchdog relays (S7) and the own-build RP2040 speed and standstill monitor (SLS-L8) are uncertified.
       • All six input groups go in series into one PNOZ X3 input pair. Series connection causes fault masking, which
          limits diagnostic coverage.
  2. No PL calculation: no MTTFd, DC, CCF or SISTEMA, and no safety-function list with the required PL per
     function. PL d is not demonstrated.
  3. EDM wiring. The feedback is drawn from KM2 only, into the reset button. It should be the NC mirror contacts
     of both KM1 and KM2 in series in the PNOZ feedback loop.
  4. Lift driver. It is not chosen or priced (sheet 13 says so).
       • The actuator draws up to 3 A, per the supplier.
       • There is no current limit or stall detection, no down limit, and no “lift only when standstill” interlock.
  5. Actuator holding. The drawing states “the screw actuator holds the bin” after a stop. The supplier page states
     no self-locking or static holding force: https://www.anodas.lt/en/fdi-linear-actuator-24v-400mm-6mm-s, fetched 14
     Nov 2026. It lists:
       • L = 620 mm, IP65, 4,000 N, 6 mm/s, maximum current 3 A;
       • “Limit switches: Not adjustable”, “Pulse resolution: -”, that is, no feedback;
       • a price of EUR 137.10.
     Whether it holds 1.1-3 kN unpowered is unverified, and with no feedback the lift height is open-loop.
  6. Power electronics.
       • There is no pre-charge for closing KM1/KM2 onto the RoboClaw’s input capacitors.
       • There is no clamp or dump for regenerated energy when the contactors open while the regenerative RoboClaw
          is braking. ASSUMED good practice; check with Basicmicro.
  7. Fuse and wire ratings are not set. The 50 A main fuse is against 2 x 30 A channels plus the lift (S1 is a slow
     MIDI).
  8. Segregation is not addressed. L9 carries the front-bumper stop loop in the same loom as the 2.5 mm2 charge
     pair and the lamp pairs. The charge contacts on the bumper face are exposed; behind the diode they are SELV, but
     they are live if the ideal diode fails short.
  9. BOM S6 still says “Actuator reversing pair KA1/KA2 DELETED (actuator on Roboteq ch3)”. There is no
     Roboteq; CTL-L8 is a 2-channel RoboClaw used by the drive.
 10. Placement. The pack heater HTR-L8 is not placed. The tilt switch, SLS and fuse box are named in the pod but
     not placed.
Fix: write a safety-function list and wiring table:
  • per-function PL with a SISTEMA estimate;
  • two-channel tilt detection, for example two switches;
  • a relay with enough monitored inputs (for example PNOZ X2.8P plus an expansion, or a small safety controller)
    instead of series chaining;
  • a specified lift driver with current limit and a standstill interlock;
  • the actuator’s holding force confirmed in writing by the supplier (QC/FDI);
  • pre-charge and regen handling, fuse and wire sizes, and loom segregation.

M-3. Fixed pad: geometry partly fixed; the spring stack cannot give 45 mm
Evidence (Fig 4; CAD at the revision 2 pose):
  • The pad face is at x 294.8 and the nib tip at x 285.0, so the pad is 9.8 mm behind the nib. The claim is 11 mm
    and the request was 10 mm or more. The sign convention is confirmed: pad_in -0.015 moved the face 10 mm from
    revision 1’s -0.005.



                                                            5
  • The clear gap from face to cup is 47 mm, but the drawn springs are 46 mm long and sit 1.2 mm from the cup.
    That means:
      – no 300 N preload (which at 30 kN/m needs 10 mm of compression);
      – no die spring of 46 mm free length can deflect 45 mm. ISO 10243 allows roughly 25-50% of free length
        (ASSUMED).
  • 45 mm at 30 kN/m plus preload is 1.65 kN on the bin wall.
  • The sim still models pad_stroke = 0.040 (from rec_B2F.json).
Fix:
  • Springs of about 120-180 mm free length in guided pockets, or a gas spring.
  • Recheck the rate so full stroke stays under about 0.6 kN.
  • Model 45 mm in the sim.
  • Test with the bin brake on and off, on Brian’s bins (still open).

  • Joint. Two 8 mm plates, each a 135 x 60 upper arm plus a 144 x 30 leg, joined to the nose cross-member on one
    edge line.
  • Sideways knock, plates as drawn and assumed welded:

  • Frontal push: SF 4.0 at 970 N and 2.15 at 1,818 N.
  • No bumper travel. BOM BMP-L8 is a bar “on sprung guides” actuating D4N switches. The brackets bolt the
    bar rigidly, so no guide, travel or switch is drawn, and the bar cannot stroke.
  • Mass. The table counts 5 mm plates; the drawing shows 8 mm.
Clear:
  • The castor swivel envelope is more than 20 mm from the brackets at roll 0 and +/-2.6 deg.
  • The brackets are 43 mm from the charge contacts.
  • L9’s charge pair runs 39 mm or more from the brackets.
Fix:
  • Design a bumper carrier: a box or tube from the cross-member, carrying sprung guides and the D4N switches, sized
    for 1 kN or more sideways.
  • Decide how docking on the bumper-face contacts avoids tripping the bumper.

       pose-generating grab. Poses were made on MuJoCo 3.17.0 in this sandbox, against their 4.0.1, which may shift the
