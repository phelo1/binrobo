                          B2-F lane physics campaign - MuJoCo digital twin of the 633 m [village] lane
                                            Results against the frozen criteria. B2-F provisional pending bin check B0.




                                                                                 2026-11-13

Digital twin simulation - not field footage. B2-F provisional pending bin check B0. Nothing on the lane has been surveyed on site. Every chainage, depth and friction
value below comes from public map/DEM data or is an assumption (see section 4).


1. Summary
  • Scenarios: P1, P3, P4, P6 PASS; P2 and P7 FAIL; P5 mixed. Scored runs: 11 PASS, 7 FAIL (plus 2 supplementary “pothole filled” runs, both FAIL).
  • The loaded missions pass: P1 (240 L, 110 kg) and P3 (140 L, 66 kg) grab at home, carry 633 m (incl. the assumed 12 % wet-leaf pitch, the 1:4 step ramp and the
    pothole) and set down at pad A. P4 (hold on 12 % wet leaves, both ways) and P6 (person e-stop, dog stop/resume) pass.
  • Everything that fails is a terrain or traction limit of the drive base, not the grab/lift/receiver:
      1. Sharp 40 mm up-step (P5d/e): drive wheels cannot climb it (needs mu 0.86, have 0.6). 4 kN lift cap does not help.
      2. Open 200 mm x 40 mm trench (P5g/i): drive wheels wedge across it, stall; bin touches ground (458/468 N). 4 kN does not help.
      3. 60 mm pothole under a drive-wheel track: passes loaded at mu 0.6 (P1, P3, P5h) but traps the wheel when the axle is lightly loaded (P2a empty tug, P2c 13 kg
         empty bin) or friction is 20 % lower (P7).
      4. Light-load slip on wet leaves (supplementary P2a_pf / P2c_pf): with no or an empty bin, a drive wheel unloads on the rigid 4-point chassis, slips, and the tug
         yaws off the lane into the hedge line on the 4-12 % wet section.
      5. Bin-wheel clearance geometry: on any 40 mm level drop the bin wheels (0.24 m behind the drive axle) dip to 13.6 mm (1:4 ramp), 2.9-3.2 mm (sharp drop) and
         11.2 mm in P7. Not a gate failure (reported, not gating, as in the frozen runner) but the margin is thin.
  • Recommended before field work: fill/ramp every edge > 25 mm on the lane (step, trench, pothole), add drive-axle load or suspension (or a rocker on the castor pair),
    add traction control / yaw-rate limiting to the drive firmware, and keep the lane clear of wet leaves on the pitch - then re-run P2/P5/P7 with those changes declared.

3. Failures and root causes
F1 - Sharp 40 mm up-step (P5d 3 kN, P5e 4 kN). End state (diag_trench.py): castors already on the upper level, both drive wheels (r = 165 mm) on the lower level
bearing on the 40 mm edge. Contact angle acos((165-40)/165) = 40.7 deg, so climbing on traction alone needs mu >= tan 40.7 = 0.86; ground mu is 0.6. The drive saturates, no
progress for 12 s -> stall. The lift actuator is not in the load path for this, which is why 4 kN changes nothing. The same step with a 1:4 ramp (P5f) passes with 47 mm
bin-wheel clearance.
F2 - Open trench 40 mm x 200 mm (P5g 3 kN, P5i 4 kN). The drive wheel chord at 40 mm depth is 2 x sqrt(165 x 165 - 125 x 125) = 215 mm, longer than the 200 mm
gap, so the wheel drops in and bears on both edges (wedged); the tug pitches back (8.7 deg), the anti-tip skids land on the near level, the drive stalls at s 455.1 and the bin dips
onto the ground (458 N / 468 N; bin wheels 16 mm at the end, not captured). Lift saturates at either cap; raising it to 4 kN does not change the outcome. (The solver reports
very large wedge contact forces in this pose; treat those as a modelling artefact - the stall is geometric.) With the trench covered flush (prepared lane, P1/P3/P7) it is a non-event.
F3 - 60 mm pothole under the drive-wheel track (P2a, P2c, P7). Pothole 0.40 x 0.25 m, 60 mm deep, centred on lat +0.40 m = exactly one drive-wheel track. Climbing
its far edge needs a 50.5 deg contact angle (traction ratio 1.21). Loaded with 110 kg / 66 kg at mu 0.6 the tug gets over it (P1, P3, P5h; lift peaks 2,210 / 1,736 N there). It fails
when:
  • the axle is light: P2a (no bin, 93 kg tug) and P2c (13 kg empty bin) - the trapped wheel holds while the other wheel drives, the tug yaws 45 -> 97 deg and the anti-tip skid
    lands on the pothole tile -> stall s 550.0 / 549.8;
  • friction is 20 % lower: P7 (mu 0.48) - the left drive wheel drops in at s 550.4 and the tug circles about it in a ~24 s limit cycle (bin still captured, ~42 mm, hedge min 0.27
    m) until the 2,400 s drive timeout.
F4 - Light-load slip and lane departure on wet leaves (supplementary P2a_pf, P2c_pf; pothole filled). Both reach the wet-leaf patch (mu 0.35, s 128-165, the
assumed 12 % pitch) climbing towards home. diag_slip.py from snapshot s 166: with no bin the drive axle carries only ~455 N in total and the load swaps side to side; at s 164.1
the left drive wheel unloads to 0 N (right 437 N). The rigid 4-point chassis (2 drive wheels + 2 castors, no suspension) rocks on the lane surface warp; on mu 0.35 the remaining
wheel’s servo torque yaws the tug, and the pure-pursuit controller has no slip/yaw-rate handling, so it slews across the lane. P2a_pf: repeated events at s 163.4, 158.7, 153.5,
149.1, then 110 deg of yaw and > 2 m outside the mapped hedge line (hedge -2.89 m), stall s 163.3. P2c_pf (13 kg bin, 582 N on the drive axle vs 1,536 N with 110 kg): one 90
deg yaw at s 149.4, footprint 1.13 m past the hedge line, then it recovered, reached home and set the bin down correctly - still a FAIL on hedge clearance. In the sim the hedge is
a 0.8 m deep band of 1 m collision boxes and the tug pushed through it; real hedge resistance is not modelled, so read this as “lane departure”.
F5 - Bin-wheel clearance on a 40 mm level drop (all outbound runs; not a gate failure). The bin wheels trail the drive axle by about 0.24 m. When the drive axle
has stepped down 40 mm the bin wheels are still over the upper level, so their 69 mm carry clearance falls by ~40 mm plus pitch dynamics: 13.6 mm on the 1:4 ramp (P1, P5c),
11.2 mm in P7 (bin CG +60 mm, mass x1.1), 2.9 mm (3 kN) / 3.2 mm (4 kN) on the sharp drop (P5a/b, lift at its cap). No ground contact occurred, so per the frozen runner
(clearance >= 30 mm gated only at the end of each carry segment) these pass. The margin is thin; a 50 mm edge would very likely touch.
Harness and model issues found and fixed during the campaign (declared before the affected re-runs; superseded runs kept): P1 v1 carry flag left on during
set-down (runs/_P1_v1_carryflag); P1 v2 1:4 ramp box rotated the wrong way (runs/_P1_v2_rampsign); P4b v1 approach speed floor too low for the kv-150 servo on 12 %
(runs/_P4b_v1_lowspeed); P6a/b obstacle-script fixes (runs/_P6a_v1_person_stays, _P6b_v1_dog_far, _P6b_v2_nolog); the 2,400 s drive timeout was dead code (fixed
during P7 before it fired; P7 continued, no criteria change).

5. Trims and declared deviations
  1. P2: the empty outbound leg home -> pad A is not simulated; the tug is placed at the docking start pose 1.2 m from the emptied bin left by P1.
  2. P4/P5/P6 (and P2c_pf) start from carried-state snapshots of an earlier run instead of a fresh grab.
  3. One seed per scenario.
  4. The 140 L bin is drawn as a scaled 240 L CAD bin in the video (physics uses the WEB140 model).
  5. Supplementary runs added after P2a/P2c failed (declared before running): P2a_pf, P2c_pf with the pothole filled. They are reported but do not replace the scored as-is
     runs.
  6. Added variant P5i (open trench, 4 kN cap). The 4 kN sharp drop is P5b.
  7. Model: revG/sim/cg/h2/simB2h.py unchanged (only the ground replaced by the lane world); frozen contact, drive, brake and firmware (grab/set_down) terms as in the
     gate. CRITERIA_FROZEN.md sha256 2cf3f4ec. . . 8a74, not edited.
  8. Videos: 3D raster at 0.75 scale upscaled to 1280x720 (HUD at full resolution); near-side hedge cut away when the camera would sit inside it (labelled on frame); time-lapse
     factors shown on every frame.
