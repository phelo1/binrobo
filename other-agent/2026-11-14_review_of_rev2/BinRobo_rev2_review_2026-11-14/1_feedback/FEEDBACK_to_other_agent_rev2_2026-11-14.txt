PART C. Blockers and majors from the drawings

B-1 (new). The lift top end does not work as drawn.
• The carriage plate (x 425-455, y to 230) runs into the top limit switch body (x 452-476, y 222-240, z 1300-1326) at q = 300 mm. That is only 10.6 mm above carry (q 289.4).
• It then hits the "345 mm" hard stops (x 446-470, z 1336-1354) at q = 336 mm, before the 340 mm switch point.
• The hard stops are rail clamps.
  - A size-15 manual rail clamp is rated 1,200 N (Zimmer HK1501A datasheet). Two give 2,400 N against a 4,000 N actuator: SF 0.60, so they slip.
  - Note that 4 kN is the rating, not the stall force.
• The actuator is 632 mm pin to pin against L = 620 mm, so its internal end stop is at q of about 388 mm. That puts the plate 18 mm into the top tie (underside 1370) if the switch fails.
• The switch is single-channel and drawn floating 4.5 mm off the upright, with no bracket.
• Fix:
  - move the switch and stops outboard of the plate (y > 232), or notch the plate;
  - use a bolted hard-stop block through the rail-backing flat into the upright, rated at least 2 x stall force;
  - land the blocks at 340 mm at most, keeping 25 mm or more under the tie;
  - preferably fit a 350 mm-stroke unit, so its internal limit is the hard limit.

M-2. Fixed pad springs.
• The pad face is 9.8 mm behind the nib, and there is a 47 mm gap face to cup.
• But the drawn springs are 46 mm long and sit 1.2 mm from the cup. That gives no 300 N preload, and no 46 mm die spring can deflect 45 mm (about 25-50% of free length).
• 45 mm at 30 kN/m plus preload is 1.65 kN on the bin wall.
• The sim still models pad_stroke 0.040.
• Fix: guided springs of about 120-180 mm free length, or a gas spring; keep full stroke under about 0.6 kN; model 45 mm.

M-3. Bumper brackets are a concept only.
• Sideways knock SF:
  - 500 N: 2.5;
  - 970 N (drive torque cap): 1.29;
  - 1,818 N (the 150 N m logged): 0.69.
• The bar is bolted rigidly, so the BMP-L8 "sprung guides" and D4N switches can never actuate. The bumper stop function does not exist as drawn.
• Fix:
  - design a carrier with sprung guides and switches, sized for 1 kN or more sideways;
  - decide how docking on the bumper-face charge contacts avoids tripping the bumper.
