     THE NEW
     BIN GENERATION
     Maximum stacking
     TRANSPORT
     SULO has been exporting bins all over the world for 40 years. Since no
     bin manufacturer in the world has the capacity to store all its bins fully
     assembled, bins are always stored and shipped in stacked form. The
     products still take up considerable space, so the main day-to-day challenge
     is to optimize use of transport space.




     OPTIMIZED TRANSPORT
