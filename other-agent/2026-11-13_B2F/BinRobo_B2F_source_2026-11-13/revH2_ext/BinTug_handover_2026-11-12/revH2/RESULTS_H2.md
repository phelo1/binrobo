# Bin Tug Rev H2: results

Generated by `sim/make_results_md.py` on 2026-11-12 from the JSON files in `results/`. **Nothing here has been built or bench-tested, and nothing here is a MuJoCo or SITL result.**
The models are first-order (`sim/dyn.py`): one rigid body with x, z, pitch and roll, penalty wheel contact and stick-slip tyre friction. The bin is welded to the robot and there is no yaw. The other agent's MuJoCo gate is the stricter test and Rev H2 has not been through it: see `docs/TESTS_AND_SIMULATIONS.md`.

## 10. What these results do not show

- Contact between comb and receiver, the bin swinging onto the pad, the bin leaving the comb, or the bin's wheels touching the ground: the bin is welded in this model. G1, G5, G6 and G7 of the frozen gate are open.
- Yaw: one-sided obstacle results are optimistic, and a jack-knife or slew on a step cannot appear.
- The full-width 40 mm step: this model passes it; the other agent's MuJoCo model stalls every design on it. Treat it as failing until a kerb test says otherwise.
- Software: no SITL run. Timings are from a kinematic model of the lane profile, checked against one Rev G SITL run.
- The real bins: front-wall position, receiver profile and lip strength are from drawings and the standard's public summaries, not from Brian's bins.
- Prices: 23 % of the donor-build parts value is price-checked; the rest are estimates or excerpts dated 11 and 12 November 2026.
